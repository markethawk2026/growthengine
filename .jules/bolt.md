## 2025-05-18 - Deduplicate Market Universe Fetches & Pre-allocate Technical Indicator Arrays
**Learning:** In composite dashboard sections (e.g., Market Intelligence UI), sub-component queries like `leaders()` often implicitly request market breadth or quotes that top-level section renders have already fetched. Threading pre-fetched breadth objects into sub-queries eliminates duplicate RTT network calls per render. Additionally, replacing array primitives (`slice`, `reduce`, `push`) in loop-heavy technical indicator functions like `calcEMASeries` with pre-allocated arrays reduces garbage collection pressure by >50%.
**Action:** Always check composite render pipelines for hidden sub-query duplicates and pre-allocate fixed-length arrays when computing numeric series in financial indicators.
